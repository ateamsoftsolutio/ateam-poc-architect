# Privacy policy: POC Architect by ATeam

Effective date: 6 October 2026

This policy covers the "POC Architect by ATeam" plugin (`ateam-poc-architect`), published by ATeam Soft Solutions.

## What the plugin is

The plugin contains only written instructions for Claude (skills) and reference notes. It has no MCP servers, hooks, scripts or other code that runs on its own. ATeam does not run any server or service for it.

## Data ATeam collects

None. The plugin has no analytics, no telemetry and no accounts. ATeam does not receive anything you or Claude do with it.

## Data the skills handle in your Claude session

When you use the skills, Claude may read material you provide, such as:

- customer use-case documents and sample inputs, which can contain personal data such as names, emails or addresses
- the names and roles of the people who confirm answers, review guardrails or approve a brief

Claude reads this material within your own Claude session. The outputs (the handover pack, architecture brief, build plan, test results and cost logs) are saved where you choose, as Claude Docs or as files in your project folder. Anthropic processes this content under its own terms and your account settings. ATeam has no access to it.

## Outside services

- **Pricing lookups:** during Stage 4, Claude may use web search or fetch, where available, to read the public pricing pages of Anthropic and OpenAI. No user data is sent to those pages.
- **The POC you build:** in Stage 7, the application you build may call AI providers' APIs with your own keys. That is your application, covered by your agreements with those providers, not by this plugin.

## Retention

ATeam keeps no data from the plugin's use, because it receives none.

## Children

The plugin is meant for professional use and is not intended for anyone under 18.

## Your responsibilities

Get the customer's permission before sharing their documents or data with Claude, and anonymise samples where you can. The plugin's compliance flags are prompts to check with the right person. They are not legal advice.

## Changes

Any change to this policy will be published in this file in the plugin's GitHub repository, with a new effective date.
