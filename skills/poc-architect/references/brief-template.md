# Architecture brief template

Create the brief as a **Claude Doc** when a docs tool is available. Otherwise create a Markdown file in the project folder. Include:

1. **Status:** POC, customer, builder, date, current stage, approval status
2. Handover pack link and any waiver
3. The builder's teach-back (verbatim)
4. The question-and-answer log and the challenge round output (all six sections)
5. The client clarification list and the answers received
6. Readiness score, open assumptions (owner and date), and research notes
7. Architecture: workflow or agent, each step's level, model, provider, reason, and the cheaper option rejected; the stack
8. Cost model with its working, and the cost-ceiling status
9. Guardrails: always-on, proposed, the builder's decisions and comments, and any flags
10. Compliance flags
11. Test plan: at least 10 cases, including edge cases, with at least half from real samples
12. Build outline, including the mid-build demo point
13. **Approval:** the Product Owner's name, date, decision and comments (left blank until supplied)
